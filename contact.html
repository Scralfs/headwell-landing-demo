Common questions

Is the assessment a diagnosis?

The free check is a screening questionnaire, not a diagnosis. A full assessment is a structured clinical process; a diagnosis is not guaranteed, as it depends on what the assessment finds.

How does the online assessment work?

Everything happens remotely by secure video call with our assessor. There's no need to travel or attend in person.

What does it cost?

Placeholder Pricing to be confirmed.

What if I'm struggling right now?

If you need to talk to someone, the Samaritans are available free, any time, on 116 123. For urgent medical help call NHS 111, and in an emergency call 999.
