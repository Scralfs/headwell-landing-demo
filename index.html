What the check is — and what it isn't

The free check is a short questionnaire about attention, focus and related everyday experiences. It's a simple screening step, not a diagnosis.

A result suggesting you might benefit from further review doesn't mean you have ADHD, and a lower result doesn't rule it out. Only a full clinical assessment can explore that properly. The check is simply a helpful, no-cost way to decide whether taking that step is right for you.
